About TIEN Ghana

No talented student should graduate without a first chance.

The Tertiary Innovation and Excellence Network is a national initiative to grow a generation of tertiary students committed to innovation, leadership and excellence.

Vision

A thriving ecosystem of tertiary students who lead innovation and drive sustainable development in Ghana and across Africa.

Mission

To empower tertiary students through structured platforms, mentorship and real opportunities to build solutions, lead, and move from campus into work or enterprise.
